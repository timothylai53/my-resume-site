Here's how a summary is produced, from the moment you open a report to the numbers on the screen.

1. The data it starts from

The summaries never read your Excel file. When you click Process, each record is enriched once with the rule outputs (Cluster, IBS, Mapped Business Function, Within SLA?, Prioty, Quarter, Month) and stored in PostgreSQL as one processing run.

A report always uses the latest completed run. That run is loaded into memory once, so changing a filter doesn't read the database again.

2. What happens when you open a view or change a filter

The page asks the backend for one report, for example:
GET /api/reports/remediation_rate?remediation_status=Open&remediation_status=In Progress
The backend then works through these steps (backend/app/reporting/service.py):

Step 1 — Filter. It keeps only the records that match every selected filter.
- Selecting several values in one filter means "any of these".
- Different filters must all match.
- An empty filter means All.
- The SLA Due Date filter keeps dates between From and To, inclusive.
- On Vulnerability Prioritization only: records with a blank Prioty are set aside unless Include Blank Priority is ticked, and the number set aside is reported.

Step 2 — Group into rows. Each remaining record is placed under its Cluster, and within it under its ICS Group Country. A blank value becomes its own row, labelled "NA" or "(blank)".

Step 3 — Group into columns. The column depends on the view:
- Vulnerability Prioritization: Prioty (P1–P4)
- Remediation Rate: Met or Breached SLA
- Remediation Plan: Quarter, and also Month underneath. Unplanned records get a quarter column only, with no month.

Step 4 — Count. Every number is a distinct count of Unique Reference ID, and each one is calculated separately from the records behind it:
- cell (e.g. ASEAN × Open): distinct IDs that are ASEAN and Open
- row total (e.g. ASEAN): distinct IDs in ASEAN, in any column
- **column total

Here's what you see on the Vulnerability Summary pages and how to use them.

Getting there

The sidebar's Vulnerability Summary section has three links, one per report:
- Vulnerability Prioritization (the default page)
- Remediation Rate
- Remediation Plan

You can also switch between them with the Report View dropdown at the top of the page.

What's on the page, top to bottom

1. Header. The report name, plus "Data as of 31 Aug 2026" and the file name of the processed dataset.

2. Report setup card
- Report View: which of the three reports you're looking at.
- Rows: Cluster → ICS Group Country (the default), Cluster only, or ICS Group Country only.
- Columns: shows what the columns are, for example Prioty.
- Metric: "Number of Vulnerability (distinct Unique Reference ID)".

3. Filters card. Each report shows only its own filters:

│         Report          │                                        Filters                                        │
├─────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Vulnerability           │ Remediation Domain Grouping, ICS Hierarchy, Within SLA?, Remediation Status, and the  │
│ Prioritization          │ Include Blank Priority tick-box                                                       │
├─────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤

- Click a filter to tick one or more values. It then shows "All", the one value you picked, or "2 selected".
- Reset filters clears them all.
- Everything below updates straight away, without reloading the page.

4. KPI cards. One card for the total Number of Vulnerability, then one card per column with its share of the total:
- Prioritization: P1, P2, P3, P4. The total card says "Excludes 562 without Prioty".
- Remediation Rate: Met SLA, Breached SLA, Exception, Open, Overdue.
- Remediation Plan: each quarter and Unplanned.

5. Chart and Distribution.
- Left: a stacked bar for each cluster, split by the report's columns, with a legend.
- Right: each column's count and percentage of the total.

6. The summary table. This is the PivotTable equivalent.
- Rows: clusters, collapsed at first. Click ▸ next to a cluster, or Expand rows, to see its ICS Group Countries. NA and Check Mapping are listed last.
- Columns: P1–P4, the SLA outcomes, or the quarters.
- On Remediation Plan: click ▸ on a quarter, or Expand months, to show its months followed by a "Quarter Total" column. Unplanned has no months.
- Grand Total is the last column and the bottom row.

7. Click any number to see its records. A panel opens from the right listing those vulnerabilities: ID, cluster, ICS Group Country, CIID, IBS, severity, priority, Within SLA?, status, Met or Breached SLA, planned month and any data-quality issues. The panel shows 50 records per page, and Esc closes it.

Things you might notice

- Prioritization shows 438, while the other two views show 1,000. That's because records with a blank Prioty are hidden by default. Tick Include Blank Priority to add them back as a "(blank)" column.
- Every number is a distinct count of IDs. When an ID appears in more than one country or category, a total can be lower than its parts added together. The table's subtitle says so.
