1. CLUSTER — IMPORTANT CORRECTION

Cluster is NOT derived from Country.

Cluster must be derived from:

ICS Group Country

using:

Raw Data[ICS Group Country]
-> Cluster Mapping[ICS Group Country]
-> Cluster Mapping[Cluster]

Rules:

Blank ICS Group Country
-> "NA"

Mapped ICS Group Country
-> mapped Cluster

Nonblank ICS Group Country not found in mapping
-> "Check Mapping"

Do NOT use Country as a fallback.

Do NOT parse Country to derive Cluster.

Therefore validation should check unmapped ICS Group Country values, not
unmapped Country values.
