Three things to confirm (behaviour I chose, not mistakes)

1. Vulnerability Prioritization shows 438 and hides 562 records whose Prioty is blank (severity not eligible). An Excel PivotTable would normally show a "(blank)" column unless it's filtered out. Should the default be hidden, as now, or shown?
2. Remediation Plan counts every status by default, including Closed and Remediated records that still have a planned date. Does your Excel report count only open items (Open / In Progress / Overdue), or everything? Today you'd need the Remediation Status filter to narrow it.

One limitation of the test data

Your current file has no duplicate Unique Reference IDs, so on this data a distinct count equals a row count. The automated tests do cover duplicates (for example "A, A, B, C = 3", and a total being lower than the sum of its children), but your real data will be the first true test of that at scale.
